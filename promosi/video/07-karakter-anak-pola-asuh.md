## 2. Kunci konsistensi karakter (WAJIB dilakukan sebelum membuat klip)

Generator video sering mengubah wajah dan pakaian antar-klip. Supaya tokoh tetap orang yang sama dari detik 0 sampai 58, lakukan langkah ini:

**Langkah A — buat lembar karakter (character sheet) sekali saja.** Jalankan prompt di bawah di generator gambar, pilih hasil terbaik, simpan sebagai `07-karakter.png`:

```text
Character reference sheet of BU MAYA: Indonesian woman, 36 years old, oval face, tan skin, black hair in a loose bun, kind tired eyes, wearing a mustard-yellow cotton top and blue jeans. Three views on a plain light-grey studio background: full-body front view, 3/4 view, and a close-up head view looking at the camera with a neutral calm expression. Even soft studio lighting, same person in every view, photorealistic, no text, no logo.
```

```text
Character reference sheet of RIZKI (child): Indonesian boy, 7 years old, round face, short black hair with a small cowlick, big dark eyes, a small gap between his front teeth, wearing a green dinosaur-print t-shirt and khaki shorts. Three views on a plain light-grey studio background: full-body front view, 3/4 view, and a close-up head view looking at the camera with a neutral calm expression. Even soft studio lighting, same person in every view, photorealistic, no text, no logo.
```

**Langkah B — kunci di setiap klip.**
1. Pakai gambar lembar karakter sebagai **gambar referensi** (fitur *image-to-video*, *character/subject reference*, atau *ingredients*; nama fitur tergantung generator). Klip pertama boleh *text-to-video*, lalu jadikan **frame terbaiknya** sebagai referensi untuk klip berikutnya.
2. Salin **blok kunci tokoh** di bawah **persis sama, kata demi kata**, di awal prompt tiap adegan yang memperlihatkan tokoh (sudah otomatis ada di prompt adegan S1, S2, S3, S6, S7). Jangan diparafrase.
3. Pakai **seed yang sama** (catat angkanya) dan setelan kamera yang sama: lensa 35 mm, tinggi mata, cahaya hangat keemasan dari samping.
4. **Pakaian tetap sama** sepanjang video (kecuali yang tertulis di blok kunci). Properti utama juga sama (kacamata, hijab, dsb.).
5. Buat **2–3 percobaan per adegan** dan pilih yang wajahnya paling mirip lembar karakter. Bila wajah melenceng, ulangi, jangan dipaksakan.
6. Hindari kerumunan dan banyak wajah dekat kamera; tokoh pendukung cukup dari belakang/siluet/blur agar wajah tokoh utama tidak rusak.
7. Di editor, samakan warna (color grade) semua klip: satu preset yang sama.

**Blok kunci tokoh:**

```text
[CHARACTER BU MAYA: Indonesian woman, 36 years old, oval face, tan skin, black hair in a loose bun, kind tired eyes, wearing a mustard-yellow cotton top and blue jeans. Identical face, hair, body and outfit as the reference image in every shot.]
[CHARACTER RIZKI (child): Indonesian boy, 7 years old, round face, short black hair with a small cowlick, big dark eyes, a small gap between his front teeth, wearing a green dinosaur-print t-shirt and khaki shorts. Identical face, hair, body and outfit as the reference image in every shot.]
```
